Burnout as a heat map

A study of medical student mental health, measured on two validated instruments and read as density rather than as points.

Medical Student Mental Health - live Open on Tableau Public

The live workbook, served by Tableau Public. It sets x-frame-options: SAMEORIGIN, so this uses Tableau's embedding API rather than an iframe - which means this panel needs a connection.

01 - Context

An entry for Chart Champ 2023

Cleartelligence held its eighth annual Chart Champ contest on 4 October 2023 at Boston University. The theme that year was higher education. Dashboards were due on 22 September, and the five highest-placed entries were presented live at the event, with $1,000 for first place.

This workbook was submitted to that contest. Its description on Tableau Public reads “Medical Student Mental Health; Heat map study / Cleartelligence Chart Champ dashboard entry”. Nothing in the workbook or in this repository records how it placed.

The subject chosen under the theme is medical student mental health: survey scores for depression, burnout and exhaustion, set against curriculum year and weekly study hours. The dashboard names no source. The plotted measure names correspond to columns in a public Kaggle dataset of the same title, whose fields include cesd, stud_h, health and the MBI subscales.

02 - Process

Building and publishing the workbook

The workbook was built in Tableau and first published to Tableau Public on 20 September 2023, two days before the submission deadline. A second publish followed on 26 September, after the deadline and before the event. The stored revision string is 3.9.

The published file is 77,410 bytes and exposes a single view, Dashboard1. Data download is turned off, so the survey table cannot be exported from Tableau Public, and no copy of it is held in this repository. The publish settings hide the sheet tabs, keep the toolbar and keep the Tableau Public watermark; the embed on this page is configured to match.

The case study page was written in commit 1320fb5. Tableau Public responds with x-frame-options: SAMEORIGIN, so the page loads Tableau's embedding API module and mounts a tableau-viz element, which the API renders in a shadow root. A local rule sets a 40rem minimum height on the wrapper, because the custom element defaults to display: inline and would collapse without it. Commit 46f72d2 later moved the page into the site's four-category structure.

03 - Design

Density marks across two panels

The canvas is 1,366 by 795 and holds two panels. On the left, severity of depression symptoms runs 0 to 60 on the y axis against curriculum year 1 to 6, drawn on a grey-to-teal ramp. On the right, three strips stack over a shared x axis of hours of study per week, 0 to 75: academic burnout at 0 to 40, emotional exhaustion at 0 to 35, and mental health at 1 to 6, all on a grey-to-orange-to-red ramp.

The marks are Tableau density marks. Enlarged, the fills are smooth kernel-smoothed gradients with no cell edges, so repeated responses at the same coordinates accumulate into continuous regions.

A pale grey silhouette of a head in profile occupies roughly the right quarter of the canvas and carries no data. The published dashboard has no title, subtitle, legend, annotation, units note or source line. Axis titles and tick labels are the only text on it.
