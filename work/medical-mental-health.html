01 — The measures

Two instruments, one grid

The dashboard reads two validated scales side by side. MBI — the Maslach Burnout Inventory, here its academic efficacy subscale — and STAI, the State-Trait Anxiety Inventory. Both are ordinal, both are collected per student, and both are the kind of measure that looks like noise as a scatter plot.

Density is the honest encoding for that. A heat map says how many students sit here, which is the question, rather than plotting every respondent and letting overlap decide what you see.

A scatter plot of survey responses shows you the range. A heat map shows you where people actually are.
