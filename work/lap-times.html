01 - Context

A season of lap times

Five track days at Thunderhill Raceway Park, West configuration, between 17 May 2025 and 12 April 2026: 115 laps across 16 sessions, logged with RaceChrono and RaceStudio. The first four days carried 22 laps each and the fifth carried 27.

The fastest dry lap of the first day was 1:39.36. The fastest of the season was 1:28.39, set on lap 112 of the last day, a difference of 10.97 seconds. The steps between one day's best lap and the next were 2.24, 3.02, 3.10 and 2.61 seconds.

The season was first published as a Tableau Public dashboard, and this page rebuilds that chart in the browser. The data behind the rebuild is a reconstruction: the track days, the session count, the 1:28.39 best and the 2:01.14 wet lap are the dashboard's own figures, while the remaining individual lap times were modelled to match its distribution and were not measured. The data file records this in its meta block, and the reconstruction stands until the original RaceChrono and RaceStudio sessions are exported.

02 - Process

Rebuilding the chart

The published dashboard supplied the anchors: 115 laps, 16 sessions, a 1:28.39 best on lap 112, a 2:01.14 wet lap. The remaining lap times were generated to fit that distribution and written to assets/data/laps.json, one record per lap with day, session, time, date and a wet flag, plus a theoretical best for each day and a meta block carrying the reconstruction note.

The chart is drawn by a module script in the page. It fetches the JSON and writes the gridlines, the day boundaries, the floor segments and the 115 circles into a 760 by 380 viewBox as SVG markup. There is no build step and no static fallback, so the page has to be served over http; the fetch handler prints that instruction in place of the source line when the file is opened directly. One bug came out of the rebuild: the m:ss helper padded seconds to a fixed five characters, which printed the one-decimal first-day best as 1:039.4. The pad width now follows the number of decimals asked for.

Colour was checked with js/color.js, which measures CIEDE2000 distance under normal vision, under Machado simulations of the three dichromacies, and in greyscale. The first dark-theme wet colour, #d7bcff, failed in greyscale at ΔE 9.9 against the near-white floor line and was replaced with #bea2fb, which measures L* 72. The closest pair now measures ΔE 10.7 in the light palette and 15.4 in the dark, greyscale in both cases.

03 - Design

Reading the plot

Each circle is one lap, plotted in the order driven. Seven of the 115 are wet and take the second series colour. The rule under each track day is that day's theoretical best: 1:38.13, 1:35.89, 1:32.81, 1:29.70 and 1:26.93. No lap in the file reaches its own day's floor, and the gap from a day's best lap down to that floor runs from 1.23 seconds on the first two days to 1.46 on the fifth.

The All laps view covers 1:25 to 2:05, the range needed to hold the wet session. The Dry only view drops those seven laps and tightens the axis to 1:25-1:45, which gives the dry cluster more of the plot height. Nothing is clipped in either view.

Each theme has its own palette, and the chart re-renders when theme.js dispatches its themechange event. The reconstruction caveat prints as the source line beneath the plot.

Marks

Radius 4 at 0.72 opacity

115 circles, one per lap. Overlapping times in a dense session stack visibly instead of merging into a single shape.

Axes

m:ss and five dated bands

Y labels are formatted m:ss with a tick every ten seconds. Dashed rules divide the x axis into one band per track day, labelled with the day number and its year and month.

Detail

Readout bar below the plot

Hover and keyboard focus write the same line into a bar under the chart: lap, session, day, date, time and condition. The bar is aria-live polite, and all 115 marks take focus.

Colour

One palette per theme

Every series colour clears 3:1 against its own page background, and the closest pair measures ΔE 10.7 in light and 15.4 in dark.
